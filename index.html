⚒ Quick Level Builder Terminal Cheats

/newmap          # Wipe the workspace and launch a blank block layout matrix grid.
/savemap [name]  # Pack up your structural custom canvas data layers onto local memory.
/map [mapname]   # Instantly spin up and boot directly into a specific game arena level.
